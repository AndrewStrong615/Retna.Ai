What this is, and is not

The 3D is a projection, not a scan. One fundus photo is flat and holds no depth. The photo is wrapped onto the back wall of a standard round eyeball, assuming it covers the angle set above. Positions across the retina are roughly right; the eye's real shape, swelling or thickness are not measured (that needs OCT). "Raise the hot spots" only lifts red areas toward you so they are easier to find.
Red = where the model found evidence for diabetic retinopathy, scaled to this eye's own strongest spot (so every eye shows some red; "peak" says how strong it really was). It shows what the model used, which is not proof that it is right. The model knows only diabetic retinopathy, no other disease.
