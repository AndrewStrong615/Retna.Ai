What this is, and is not

The shape is measured. Every point of the surface comes from the OCT scan, in micrometres. "Stretch the depth" only makes the relief easier to see; at 1x the proportions are true.
The surfaces are found by a classical method (strongest dark-to-bright edges, joined into smooth lines), not by a trained model. It can fail in shadows, very swollen retina, or at the optic disc. Check the lines on the B-scan. The lower line is the top of the bright band, so thickness reads some tens of micrometres lower than a device's own number.
Red = thicker than the threshold, which by default means clearly thicker than the rest of this scan. There is no database of normal eyes behind it. It is a measurement, not a diagnosis.
No AI heat map here. The disease model reads fundus photos and has never seen OCT. Give oct3d.py a photo of the same eye (--photo, --heat) to lay its findings onto this surface.
